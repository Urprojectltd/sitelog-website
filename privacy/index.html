Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Who we are

Site Log is a mobile app provided by UrProject Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16336005), 20 Wenlock Road, London, N1 7GU ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Questions about this policy or your data: contact@sitelog.app

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data the Site Log app and this website collect, why, who else handles it, and your rights. It is written to meet the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

3. What we collect

Your account. Your email address and a password (stored only in scrambled form), and the name you give.

What you put on your reports. Your name, company name, the name shown as auditor, your company logo and your signature, if you add them.

Your site logs. Projects (name, reference, client, address), photos, the numbered marks you place on them, the items on each list with their trade, priority, status and who they are assigned to, any notes you type, and a written copy of what you said.

Voice recordings. When you record a walk, the recording is kept on your phone until you tap Generate. It is then sent to be turned into text and written up as your list. We do not keep the recording on our servers after that. The app asks for your permission the first time, and you can type the items yourself instead.

Subscription status. If you subscribe to Site Log Pro, Apple or Google takes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. We receive whether your subscription is active, what was bought and when.

How much you have used. A count of the walks written up for your account each month, so that plan limits can be applied.

Technical records. Our servers record the internet address and the time of each request for a short period, to keep the service secure and to find faults.

What we do not collect. The app does not ask for or store your location. It re-saves each photo before uploading it, which removes the location details a camera may add. In the rare case that a photo cannot be re-saved, it is uploaded as it is. The app has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

4. Why we use it, and the legal basis

Data Why Legal basis
Account details To create your account, sign you in and write to you about the service Contract
Details on your reports To put them on the PDF reports you create Contract
Site logs and photos To store your logs and build your reports Contract
Voice recordings and typed notes To write up your walk as a numbered list Consent, which the app asks for before the first one is sent
Subscription status To give you the plan you have paid for Contract
Usage count To apply plan limits and prevent misuse Legitimate interests
Technical records To keep the service secure and working Legitimate interests

You can withdraw consent at any time by not using Generate, or by turning off microphone access in your phone's settings. Typing the items yourself needs no consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything already done.

5. Who else handles your data

We use the following companies to run the service. They act on our instructions and only handle what is needed.

Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Your account, logs and photos are stored on its servers in London, United Kingdom.

OpenAI turns your voice recordings and typed notes into text and writes up the list. Your photos are not sent to OpenAI. OpenAI states that it does not use data sent this way to train its models, and that it may keep it for up to 30 days to check for misuse before deleting it. OpenAI is based in the United States.

RevenueCat tells the app whether your subscription is active. It receives an account number that we generate, your purchase records from Apple or Google, and basic details of your device and app version. It does not receive your name or email address from us. RevenueCat is based in the United States.

Resend sends our emails, such as the code that confirms your email address. It receives your email address. Resend is based in the United States.

Apple and Google process subscription payments under their own privacy policies.

GitHub hosts this website. It records the internet address of each visitor for security. It has no part in the app.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

6. Transfers outside the UK

OpenAI, RevenueCat and Resend process data in the United States, as does GitHub for visits to this website. Where data leaves the UK we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as the UK's adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses approved by the Information Commissioner's Office.

7. How long we keep it

Data How long
Account details, report details, site logs and photos Until you delete them or delete your account
Voice recordings On your phone until the walk is saved or discarded. Not kept on our servers
The written-up reply for a walk Kept so that a retry gives the same answer. Cleared the next time you send a walk more than a day later, and when you delete your account
Usage count Until you delete your account
Subscription records For as long as RevenueCat, Apple and Google keep them under their own policies
Technical records Up to 90 days

When you delete your account, your logs, photos, profile, logo, signature and usage records are deleted from our live systems straight away. Copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time:

  1. Open Site Log and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This cannot be undone. If you cannot use the app, email contact@sitelog.app from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel that in your Apple or Google account settings. See also sitelog.app/delete-account.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can read only its own data. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you and the regulator if a breach puts you at risk.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; limit how we use it; give it to you in a form you can move elsewhere; and stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time.

Email contact@sitelog.app. We reply within one month.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

12. Children

Site Log is a work tool and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

13. Cookies

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics. The app does not use cookies.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email.
