Delete your Site Log account

Site Log, by UrProject Ltd

In the app

  1. Open Site Log and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app

Email contact@sitelog.app from the email address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in details, your profile, company logo and signature, all your projects, logs, items and photos, and the record of how much you have used.

What is kept

Copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep records of any purchases under their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Site Log Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
